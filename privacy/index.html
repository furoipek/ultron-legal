Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Controller

Furkan Ipek. E-mail: furoipek.ai@gmail.com ("the Operator"). ULTRON is a private assistant used only by the Operator. It does not collect data about other people.

2. Data processed in connection with TikTok

When the Operator connects a TikTok account, the Service processes:

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by TikTok after the Operator signs in and grants permission;
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok for the connected account, limited to the approved scopes:
    • user.info.basic: read the basic profile of the connected account (account identifier, display name and avatar).
    • video.upload: send a video to the Operator's TikTok drafts so the Operator can finish and post it in the TikTok app.
  • Content the Operator chooses to post: the video file, title or caption, hashtags, and the upload status that TikTok returns.

The Service does not read other people's accounts, followers, messages or videos.

3. Purpose

The data is used only to send or publish the Operator's own videos to the Operator's own TikTok account, after the Operator's explicit approval, and to show the result of each upload to the Operator.

4. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • ULTRON runs on the Operator's Windows computer and on a virtual server of Hetzner Online GmbH in Falkenstein, Germany (Hetzner Cloud, region eu-central).
  • Tokens are stored encrypted: on the Windows computer with the Windows data-protection interface (DPAPI), and on the server with systemd encrypted credentials. They are not stored in source code or in any public repository.
  • Videos and their records are stored on those systems and sent to TikTok only for an upload that the Operator approved.
  • Approval requests and status messages are delivered to the Operator through the Telegram messaging service.
  • Titles and captions that the Operator wants to improve may be processed by AI providers (such as OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter). TikTok access tokens and TikTok account data are never sent to them.

5. Recipients

TikTok data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. It may be passed on only to the following recipients, and only as far as needed for the purposes in section 3:

  • TikTok, to carry out an upload that the Operator approved;
  • Hetzner Online GmbH as hosting provider of the server (processing on behalf of the Operator);
  • Telegram, which delivers approval requests and status messages to the Operator;
  • AI providers (such as OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter), only for titles and captions that the Operator asks to improve. They never receive TikTok access tokens or TikTok account data.

Some of these recipients may process data outside the European Economic Area.

6. Legal basis

Where data-protection law applies, the processing is based on the Operator's legitimate interest in running a private assistant that publishes the Operator's own content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for the connection to TikTok, on the permission the Operator grants in TikTok's authorisation screen (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The Operator is the only user; no data of third parties is collected for ULTRON's own purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the Operator disconnects TikTok in ULTRON or the token expires. On disconnect they are deleted from the Service.
  • Upload records are kept in the Operator's own database until the Operator deletes them.
  • To request deletion, write to furoipek.ai@gmail.com. Content already published on TikTok is managed by the Operator in the TikTok app.

8. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time inside ULTRON ("disconnect") and in the TikTok app, in the settings for connected apps and permissions.

9. Your rights

If personal data about you is processed, you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, write to furoipek.ai@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen (lfd.niedersachsen.de).

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when ULTRON or the platforms it works with change. The date above shows the latest version.
