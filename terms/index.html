1. What ULTRON is

ULTRON ("the Service") is a private, personal AI assistant operated by Furkan Ipek ("the Operator") and intended for use by the Operator only. There are no user accounts for third parties, and the Service is not offered to the public.

2. Scope of use

The Operator uses the Service to organise tasks, prepare video content and, only after the Operator's explicit approval for each action, to publish content to social media accounts that the Operator owns or is authorised to manage.

3. Connection to TikTok

  • The Service connects to TikTok through OAuth 2.0 and the TikTok Content Posting API. The connection is established only when the Operator signs in to TikTok and grants permission.
  • The Service requests only these permissions (scopes):
    • user.info.basic: read the basic profile of the connected account (account identifier, display name and avatar).
    • video.upload: send a video to the Operator's TikTok drafts so the Operator can finish and post it in the TikTok app.
  • The Service does not publish, delete or change content on TikTok without the Operator's explicit approval.
  • The Operator can revoke access at any time (section 6).
  • Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own terms and policies. The Service is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok.

6. Ending the connection
